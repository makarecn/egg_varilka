Facts & Recipes 🥚

Facts:

  • Eggs are a complete protein containing all essential amino acids.
  • Shell color depends on the hen breed (white or brown — equally nutritious).
  • The world's largest egg was 23 cm in diameter, laid in 2010.
  • Eggs help your body absorb vitamins from vegetables better.
  • The yolk is actually one giant single cell!

Simple Recipes:

  • Scrambled Eggs: Crack 2 eggs into a pan, cook 3 min. Add salt.
  • Omelette: Whisk 2 eggs with milk, fry with veggies for 5 min.
  • Poached: Crack egg into boiling water with vinegar, cook 3 min.
  • Deviled Eggs: Hard-boil, halve, mix yolk with mayo & spices.
